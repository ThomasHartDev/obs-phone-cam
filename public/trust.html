One time per iPhone / iPad

Stop the Safari warning

This page is plain http, so it will not warn. Install the profile, then turn on full trust. After that the usual https camera and board links stay the same and Safari stays quiet.

1. Install Phone Cam profile

  1. Safari downloads a profile. Open Settings. Tap Profile Downloaded (or General → VPN & Device Management) and Install.
  2. Then Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings. Enable Phone Cam.
  3. Go back and scan the usual camera / board QR. No warning.
